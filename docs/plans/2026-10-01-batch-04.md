COS-11 may prepare its failure handoff and API design in parallel, but its implementation and retry policy must use the actual COS-10 result. Later recovery, parallel scheduling and full benchmark work keep their existing dependencies. Passed checks are reused unless the relevant code, requirements, environment or artifact changes.
